The details

What we fly

Live broadcast

Real-time video from an FPV drone to your screens, stream or venue display.

Sports FPV

Close, fast shots of racing, stunts and action sports that a normal drone cannot get.

Indoor flight

Arenas, venues and warehouses. Smooth moves through tight spaces.

Event promos

Short, punchy edits for social media and sponsors, made from the footage we shoot.

Custom builds

Every drone is built around your problem, not picked off a shelf. Tell us what you need to film or reach, and we design the frame, camera and flight setup to match.

How it works

  1. Tell us the date. Send the event, place and what you want to see.
  2. We plan it. Flight plan, safety plan and permissions are handled by us.
  3. We fly. Live feed on the day, footage delivered after.

Included

  • Pilot and custom-built FPV drone
  • Flight and safety planning
  • Live feed output (HDMI or stream, edit to match your gear)
  • Raw 4K footage within 48 hours
  • Liability insurance certificate on request

Safety

  • Transport Canada Advanced Operations pilot, drones registered
  • SFOC-RPAS obtained for advertised events, with flight zones agreed with organizers
  • NAV CANADA authorization when flying in controlled airspace
  • Pre-flight checks, failsafe return-to-home, backup drone and batteries on site
  • Liability insurance, certificate on request
  • Our drones are built in-house and declared to Transport Canada under Standard 922.05 (operations near people) and 922.06 (operations over people).

FAQ

How far ahead should we book?

Six weeks or more for public events, because event permits can take up to 30 working days.

Can you travel?

Yes. Travel costs are added to the quote.

What if the weather is bad?

We reschedule or refund the flight portion if conditions are unsafe.

Do we own the footage?

Yes, for the uses agreed in your quote.

About

I'm Bennett, a mechanical engineer who loves to build and test drones. I'm also a Transport Canada Advanced Operations pilot, so the person who designs your drone is the one who flies it.

I build each drone around the problem, not off a shelf. That has meant 180 km/h on a track, under a desk indoors, and through a chimney. I test every build hard before it goes near your event.

What I enjoy most is helping people see their world from a different perspective. Tell me what you want to see, and I'll build the drone that gets you there.
